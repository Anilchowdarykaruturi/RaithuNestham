RaithuNestham Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

RaithuNestham helps farmers access agricultural information, including weather, crops, fertilizers, pesticides, government schemes, and AI-assisted farming guidance. We aim to respect users' privacy and limit personal information collection to what is necessary for the services provided.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, RaithuNestham may process account information such as an email address, account identifier, or name provided during registration. Information entered into AI chat or other features may be transmitted to our servers to respond to a user's request.

If users manually select a village or location for weather or farming information, that selection may be processed to provide relevant results. The app should not require device location unless that feature is specifically offered and disclosed.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide requested agricultural information, respond to farming questions, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. AI Features and Service Providers

Questions submitted to AI-assisted features may be processed by our backend and an AI service provider to generate responses. External providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should avoid entering passwords or sensitive personal information into AI chat.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, databases, weather information, or AI functionality, where applicable. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. Secure connections should be used when information is transmitted to our online services. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and security requirements. Users who want to request account or personal data deletion can contact us using the email below. We will explain any information that must be retained and why.

7. Children's Privacy

RaithuNestham is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
