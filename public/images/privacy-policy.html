RaithuNestham Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

RaithuNestham is designed to help farmers access farming information, including weather, crops, fertilizers, pesticides, government schemes, and AI-assisted farming guidance. We aim to protect users' privacy and collect only the information needed to operate the service.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the service may process account information such as an email address, a user identifier, and a name if you provide one. Information you enter into AI chat or other features may also be transmitted to our servers to provide the requested service.

If you choose a village or location manually to receive relevant weather or farming information, that selection may be processed to provide the requested information. Do not enter sensitive personal information into the app.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide farming and weather information, answer farming questions, maintain service security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. AI Services and Service Providers

Questions submitted to AI-assisted features may be sent to our backend and an AI service provider to generate a response. Such providers may process submitted information under their own terms and privacy practices. We will not intentionally send personal information to an AI provider unless it is needed for the requested service or otherwise disclosed.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, database, weather, or AI providers, where applicable. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Storage and Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. Data transmitted to our online services should use secure connections where supported. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain accounts, meet applicable legal obligations, or resolve security issues. To request deletion of your account or associated personal information, contact us using the details below. We will explain any information that must be retained and why.

7. Children's Privacy

RaithuNestham is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
